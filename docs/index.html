Workflow

This Week keeps your commitments visible.

No infinite backlog. Just the work you actually expect to move this week.

Today becomes your working lane.

Pull only what deserves your attention now. The rest can wait without disappearing.

Done gives you proof, not clutter.

Completed tasks stay visible long enough to feel progress, then quietly archive away.

Why It Feels Different

Built for people who think in weeks, work in days, and ship in hours.

Today is deliberately narrow. It favors clear structure, strong shortcuts, and plain text files over endless configuration and list sprawl.

  • Monthly goals stay pinned to the top as your north star.
  • Global ghost capture lets you catch work from anywhere on your Mac.
  • Focus mode turns one task into a full-screen working surface with a timer.
  • Tasks live in `today.md`, so your data stays readable and portable.

Install

Download the release and be working in minutes.

Most people should download the latest .dmg from GitHub Releases, drag Today.app into Applications, and open it from there.

Open Releases Read install guide
  1. Download the latest Today-*.dmg from Releases.
  2. Open the DMG and drag Today.app into Applications.
  3. Launch it from Applications.
  4. If macOS blocks the first open, right-click the app and choose Open.
